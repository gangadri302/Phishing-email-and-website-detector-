⚡ How the Detection System Works

The system extracts suspicious indicators from the provided email or URL, assigns risk points and displays a security classification.

01 — INPUTEmail content or website URL
02 — ANALYZEExtract phishing indicators
03 — RISK SCORECalculate risk level
04 — RESULTShow security warning

🔐 Detection Features

Suspicious words, urgency patterns, unusual URL structures, insecure HTTP, IP-based URLs, excessive subdomains, sensitive keywords and organization-claim patterns are checked by the prototype.

⚠️ Prototype Notice

This is a rule-based academic prototype. A low-risk result does not guarantee that an email or website is completely safe.
